A diary for every milk tea, latte and lemonade you buy. Snap it, rate it, and remember next time whether you liked it.

  • Tap the logo to sip it up (sound on)
  • Press + and photograph a drink on a plain background
  • Hold the cup to sip-rate it (in pearls), toss in sugar cubes
  • Pull down at the top of a page, or tap Pick for me
  • Print a receipt in Archive, collect pearls on Shelf

Sample drinks are drawn stand-ins. Entries, photos and tweaks you make stay in this browser.
