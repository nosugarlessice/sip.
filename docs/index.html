How it works

Snap it, then sip to rate it

Take a photo and sip. cuts your drink out like a sticker, then presses it onto today. Hold the cup to rate it: the longer you sip, the more you liked it.

A stamp card for every shop

Each drink adds a pearl to that shop’s card. Ten fill a card and a fresh one starts on the next. Keep coming back and the shop levels up, from 1 to 5.

A receipt for every month

Pull a receipt out of the slot: every cup, your sip of the month, and where you went most.

Can’t decide? Shake for one

Tap Pick for me, or just shake your phone. sip. picks a drink you rated well and would order again. Shake again, or log it in one tap.

Get sip.

Free on both. Your drinks stay on your phone.

iPhone

sip. installs from Safari as a web app: it gets its own icon, opens full screen and works offline. No App Store needed.

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open sip. below.
  2. Tap Share at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open sip. from your home screen, like any app.

Android

A real app you install from the file. Android asks once because sip. isn’t in the Play Store yet.

  1. Tap Download .apk and open the file from the notification or Downloads.
  2. If Android says your browser can’t install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap More details → Install anyway.
